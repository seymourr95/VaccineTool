*Distemper = the DA2PP / 5-way vaccine (Distemper, Adenovirus I & II, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza). Distemper and Leptospirosis are given as a single combined vaccine whenever both fall on the same visit; the one distemper dose given without leptospirosis uses a standalone distemper vaccine.  **Rabies: renewal depends on state laws and risk.
The first visit is set to exactly 8 weeks and the final puppy visit to exactly 16 weeks (the source schedule lists a 14–16 week range). The "every 3 years" toggle changes only the adult schedule — Rabies & Distemper boost at year 1 then every 3 years, while Leptospirosis stays annual; the puppy series is unaffected. Leptospirosis is a two-dose series given 3–4 weeks apart; use the selector to start it at 8 weeks or 10–12 weeks, and the second dose is placed at the next visit accordingly. Deworming, heartworm testing/prevention, microchipping, and spay/neuter timing are general wellness guidelines and vary by breed, region, and your vet's advice. This tool is a planning aid based on the petMD general guideline — always confirm timing with your licensed veterinarian.
